Key Features & Capabilities

  • High-Speed In-Memory Clipboard: Cut, copy, and paste ranges—or transpose complex 2D data matrices instantly—using an optimized memory-staging engine.
  • Advanced Cell Formatting Suite: Access custom border controls, cell shading, text direction, slant alignments, and number formats directly from a dedicated dialog box.
  • Instant Structural Controls: Insert rows and columns, adjust precise pixel heights or widths, and auto-fit cell content with a single click.
  • Layout & Alignment Toggles: Switch text alignments (horizontal and vertical), toggle text wrapping, hide/unhide specific ranges, or convert entire sheets between Right-to-Left (RTL) and Left-to-Right (LTR) views.
  • 1-Click WhatsApp™ & SMS™ Messaging: Launch pre-filled WhatsApp™ conversations or native SMS™ messages directly from selected recipient phone numbers without ever leaving your sheet™.
