Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the SidebarR5 for Sheets™ ("Add-on"). We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining a minimal-data footprint.

2. Data Collection and Usage

The Add-on runs functions strictly on the active Sheet™ directly within your user session to format cells and manipulate data. Specifically:

  • No External Transmission: None of your sheet™ data, personal information, or document contents are collected, stored, or transmitted to any external third-party server.
  • Active Sheet™ Scope: All 38 custom utility functions operate entirely within the scope of your active Google Sheet™ session.
  • WhatsApp™ Integration: The WhatsApp™ action generates a local web link (`https://web.whatsapp.com/send`) to initiate messages via your own browser. No contact data or message payload is saved or intercepted by us.

3. Permissions and Scopes

The Add-on requests standard Google Workspace™ permissions (such as access to active sheets™) solely to execute sheet™ formatting commands on your command.
